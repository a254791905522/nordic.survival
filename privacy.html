Nordic Survival — Privacy

How this Viking action adventure handles information on your device and through the App Store.

No in-app data collection Offline play supported No account required

At a glance

According to the App Store “App Privacy” label for Nordic Survival, the developer does not collect data from you through the app as Apple defines collection. Gameplay progress and settings are handled on your device unless a future update states otherwise.

Who this is for

Players and parents reviewing a 4+ rated adventure game before install. This page is a plain-language companion—not legal advice—and does not replace Apple’s store terms or mandatory regional disclosures.

Local gameplay data

Nordic Survival can store information such as level progress, crafted gear state, currency, and control preferences locally in the app’s sandbox on your iPhone or iPad. That data helps you continue a run without signing in.

Deleting the app or clearing its data through iOS Settings removes that local storage. Uninstalling is the surest way to wipe on-device game files.

What we do not collect through the game

The current App Store privacy summary states that data is not collected from the app. Nordic Survival is designed for offline play and does not require you to create a separate in-game account to enjoy the core adventure.

If a future version adds analytics, cloud saves, social features, or similar services, the App Store privacy label and this page should be updated to describe those practices.

Apple’s role

When you download or update Nordic Survival from the App Store, Apple processes information related to your Apple ID, device, and purchases under Apple’s own privacy policy. That processing is outside the game’s direct control.

For device-wide privacy choices, open Settings → Privacy & Security on your iPhone or iPad.

Children and family devices

The game is rated 4+. If younger players share a family device, use Apple’s Screen Time and parental controls for installs, in-app purchases, and usage limits in addition to reading this overview.

Your choices
  • Remove the app to delete local game data from the device.
  • Review the live App Store listing for the latest “App Privacy” section after each update.
  • Adjust iOS system permissions if future builds request camera, microphone, or other access—none are required for the current offline adventure described on the store.
Changes to this policy

This privacy overview may be revised when the game or its data practices change. When in doubt, trust the behavior of the installed build and the privacy label shown on the App Store over any older copy of this page.

Product inquiries about privacy can be directed through the support channel listed on the App Store product page for Nordic Survival.
