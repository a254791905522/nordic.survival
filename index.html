Forge weapons, gather resources, fight monsters, and survive a harsh Nordic world. Nordic Survival is a fast 3D action adventure set in a wild Viking-inspired land. Explore, craft equipment, and battle dangerous enemies while you collect wood, metal, coins, and potions. Every run is about survival, timing, and smart resource management.

How to survive

  1. Scout the wild — Search the environment for wood, metal, coins, and supplies.
  2. Craft your gear — Build shields, daggers, swords, and hammers at the forge.
  3. Fight smart — Block melee strikes, throw daggers from range, and break objects for loot.
  4. Stay alive — Use potions to recover health and push forward before the wilderness wins.
